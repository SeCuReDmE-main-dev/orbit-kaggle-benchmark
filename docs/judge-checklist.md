The published criteria are insights shared, writing quality and creativity in approach. Reaction count is a tie-breaker, not the primary benchmark score. No minimum number of models, tasks or repetitions is stated on the challenge page.

The measurement argument is deliberately bounded: a six-question native contract result, a separate 36-question deterministic replay, and separately identified historical C/C4, D and E evidence. Their denominators cannot be combined into one accuracy claim. The user's decision to retain all three engines is a product judgment.

The DEV article has not been published by this delivery. Public technical artifacts do not by themselves constitute a submitted challenge entry.
