## Objective and boundaries

Deliver the approved isolated Kaggle package with robust successor diagnostics, a reproducible reader path, accurate evidence labels and no unresolved actionable review findings. Original Orbit/Sanity is frozen. No new provider calls, model campaigns, paid reviews or DEV publication. The historical pilot, engines, corpora and receipts retain their exact bytes; their inventory is [frozen-history.json](../metadata/frozen-history.json).

The hardened successor remains prepared, locally tested and not executed/published on Kaggle. Real hosted observations remain attached to their historical source identities. ## Acceptance and workflow

Task Router maps capabilities; native Codex agents have separate code/reproducibility responsibilities. Tests cover malformed JSON, UTF-8, output schemas, diagnostic failures, no automatic retries, deterministic generation, replay-result validation and read-only package checks. CI has read-only permissions and no model credentials. Article replacement and its existing Drive copy require explicit author approval. A single branch/PR carries corrections and free reviewer rounds; ordinary merge is authorized only after applicable gates pass.

Qodo, Codex and CodeRabbit are requested only through verified free/included capacity. Install the official signed per-user Windows CodeRabbit CLI if needed. No paid overages. Silence, skipped files and quota failure are not a successful review. Every finding receives a verified correction or an evidence-based disposition.

## Tracking contract

Statuses: todo, in_progress, blocked, done, cancelled, not_applicable. `done` means **FAIT**, with actual UTC `completed_at` and evidence. An unknown historical completion time stays unknown; a verification time is recorded separately. Keep stable IDs, append related questions as suffixes (for example KAG-023.a), and retain reopening/replacement history. Capture substantive user questions without converting a note into authority for unrelated work. Never put private messages or personal filesystem paths in this public plan.

Final push/review/merge/readback times occur after the last source commit: their authoritative completion events are in the linked PR closeout receipt. This plan links that receipt rather than creating a recursive sequence of timestamp-only commits. NotaBene follows only after KAG-063 and is implemented in its own plugin source, not in this benchmark.
